Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

RondappsClipper respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use RondappsClipper.

2. Information We May Process

RondappsClipper may process information necessary to provide its services, including video files, captions, social media account authorization information, and technical information required for publishing content.

3. Video Content

Videos and captions submitted by users may be processed by RondappsClipper for the purpose of distributing content to social media platforms authorized by the user.

4. TikTok

When a user connects a TikTok account, RondappsClipper uses TikTok's authorized APIs to provide content publishing functionality. RondappsClipper only requests permissions that are necessary for the features provided by the application.

TikTok account authorization can be revoked by the user through the appropriate account or application settings provided by TikTok.

5. Data Storage

Uploaded video files may be temporarily stored while they are being processed or published. We do not sell personal information or video content to third parties.

6. Data Sharing

Information and content may be transmitted to third-party social media platforms when the user has authorized RondappsClipper to publish content through those platforms.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by RondappsClipper. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain the service, or comply with applicable requirements.

9. User Rights

Users may request information about the data handled by RondappsClipper and may request deletion of applicable data, subject to technical and legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the RondappsClipper administrator.
